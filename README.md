# Screen AI for Windows — Release v1.1.1

Screen AI is an ultra-lightweight, floating desktop companion powered by Google Gemini. Designed to stay right where you work so you can ask questions, analyze screenshots instantly with `Ctrl+V`, or talk hands-free in real-time—without interrupting your workflow or switching browser tabs.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

* **🪟 Always-on-Top Floating Canvas:** Stays conveniently pinned above your code editor, browser, or documents. Move it anywhere on your screen or minimize it with a click.
* **🗣️ Real-Time Voice Conversations:** Fluid, hands-free conversational voice mode. Speak naturally and get instant spoken answers while your hands stay on your keyboard.
* **📸 Instant Screenshot Analysis (`Ctrl+V`):** Take a quick snippet of any error message, chart, document, or design and paste it straight in to get answers immediately.
* **🎧 Meeting & Video Audio Summarizer:** Capture and summarize discussions from Zoom, Google Meet, YouTube tutorials, or webinars with a single click.
* **🔒 Privacy-First Design:** Zero continuous screen recording and zero background surveillance. The app only analyzes the exact screenshots you intentionally paste or when you start a voice call.
* **🔑 Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK):** Connect your free Google Gemini API key from Google AI Studio. Never get locked into paying expensive $20–$30 monthly subscriptions for an AI wrapper.

---

## 💻 System Requirements

* **Operating System:** Windows 10 (version 1903+) or Windows 11 (64-bit architecture)
* **Processor & Memory:** 64-bit Intel / AMD processor; 4 GB RAM minimum
* **Audio:** Standard speakers/headphones and microphone for voice interactions
* **Internet:** Active internet connection and a free Google AI Studio API key

---

## 🚀 Quick Installation Guide

1. Download the installer: **`Screen-AI-Setup.exe`** from the **Assets** section below.
2. Double-click the file to launch the setup. Installation takes under 10 seconds.
3. Launch Screen AI from your Start Menu or Desktop shortcut.
4. Enter your personal Google Gemini API key (obtainable free from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/)).
5. You're all set! Press `Win + Shift + S` to snip anything on your screen, paste with `Ctrl + V`, or tap the microphone to start talking.
